Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Endless Highway Racer ("the app") does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account or login is required.
  • The app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app uses your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) only to steer the game. This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • Game files and music are stored inside the app.
